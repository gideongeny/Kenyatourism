Privacy Policy & Data Management

Last updated: June 2026
📋

Overview

Tembea Kenya ("the App") is a Kenya tourism guide developed by Gideon Geng. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

📊

Data We Collect

  • Email address (when signing in with Google or Email)
  • Display name and profile photo (from your Google account)
  • Saved favourite destinations (stored to your account)
  • App usage analytics (anonymous, via Firebase Analytics)
  • Device information (OS version, device model) for crash reporting
🎯

Why We Collect It

  • To save and sync your favourite destinations across devices
  • To personalise your experience inside the app
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs
  • We do NOT sell your data to third parties
  • We do NOT use your data for advertising profiling
🔐

How We Protect Your Data

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS encryption)
  • User data is stored securely in Google Firebase Firestore
  • Firebase enforces authentication before any data access
  • We follow Google Play's data safety guidelines
🤝

Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase — Authentication, Database, Analytics
  • Google AdMob — In-app advertisements
  • OpenStreetMap — Map tiles (no personal data shared)
👤

Your Rights

  • Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Request deletion of specific data without deleting your account
  • Use the app as a guest (anonymous) without creating an account

To exercise any of these rights, use the Delete My Data tab above.

Request Account or Data Deletion

Fill out the form below. We will process your request within 30 days and send you a confirmation email once complete.
